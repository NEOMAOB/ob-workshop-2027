About the workshop

Welcome to Rouen

We invite you to the European OB Research Workshop at NEOMA Business School’s Rouen campus on Wednesday, 12 May 2027. Following the first edition at ESSEC in 2026, we look forward to welcoming colleagues to Rouen for this year’s workshop.

This one-day event brings together Organizational Behaviour scholars across Europe to share their research and exchange constructive, developmental feedback. It is also an opportunity to get to know colleagues at other institutions, identify shared research interests, and explore potential collaborations.

Research themes

We welcome research across the field of organizational behaviour

Including, but not limited to:

01

Leadership, motivation, work engagement, and employee well-being

02

Identity, diversity, equity, inclusion, and relationships at work

03

Teams, collaboration, emotions, and decision-making

04

Ethics, employee voice, and responses to organizational change

05

Technology, artificial intelligence, and changing ways of working

How to take part

Two presentation formats

20-minute presentation

Presentation sessions

Submit an extended abstract for a 20-minute presentation.

  • Up to 5 pages, 12-point font, double-spaced, excluding references
  • Authors of accepted abstracts may share a longer draft or full paper for circulation before the workshop (optional)
  • Submissions from non-tenured faculty will be given priority
5-minute idea kernel

Early-stage “popcorn ideas”

Share very early-stage ideas, before data collection, in round-table sessions.

  • Each presenter introduces an “idea kernel” in 5 minutes
  • Followed by discussion and feedback in small groups to help develop the idea
Attending without presenting? You are very welcome. Please indicate this on your application.

Getting there from Paris

  • Train: direct trains from Paris Saint-Lazare to Rouen Rive-Droite (Rouen central station), about 1 hr 15. From Paris airports, first travel into Paris to reach Saint-Lazare.
  • Bus from the station: about 15 minutes to campus. Take bus line 8 (towards Longs Vallons or École Moulin, Notre-Dame-de-Bondeville) to the stop Business School.
  • TEOR T1 (towards Mont aux Malades, Mont-Saint-Aignan) to the stop Place Colbert, a short walk from campus.
  • Taxi: about 20 minutes from Rouen station.
  • Car: about 1 hr 15 from Paris.

Check times on the Rouen transport network (Réseau Astuce) before travelling.
